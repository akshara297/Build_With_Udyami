RiceGuard

A concept for helping basmati rice exporters identify potential destination-market compliance risks before shipments are sent.

The proposed approach

Explore data and rule-based checks to flag potential risks early.

Current status

Concept stage. The application and planned technology stack have not been built or validated yet.
